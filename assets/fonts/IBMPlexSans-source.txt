The native renderer uses Regular for data/body text, Medium for commands and
column headings, and SemiBold for pane titles. The CPU raylib font tests check
tabular digit advances in every cut at eight raster sizes. Native atlas, DPI,
zoom, glyph appearance, and startup/render costs require windowed validation.
