## Consequences for the next implementation

Parquet browsing is already useful at this scale. CSV scan/inference dominates latency and deep OFFSET remains a real cost even though rendering continues on a separate thread. An explicit, user-directed CSV-to-Parquet workflow is a useful later feature; the app must not silently duplicate sources. Workbook persistence was the next priority at this baseline and is now implemented, as described below. Distinct header filtering and independently placed worksheet objects are the next product work.
